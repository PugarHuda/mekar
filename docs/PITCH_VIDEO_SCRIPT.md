# Pitch Video Script — Mekar (~2:30, optional/bonus)

The pitch video is the **story + vision** — distinct from the mandatory
demo video (which shows the working product). This one sells *why Mekar
matters*. It may use the `/slides` deck as visuals — slides are fine
here (unlike the demo video).

## Recording setup

- Talking-head and/or screen-record the `/slides` deck (`mekar.vercel.app/slides`,
  arrow keys to advance, F11 fullscreen).
- Narrate in **English**. Warm, confident, unhurried.
- Target ~2:30. No hard cap, but keep it tight — under 3:00.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Visual | Voiceover (read aloud) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:18 | Slide 1 (cover) or talking head. | "In 1999, Napster broke music — anyone could copy a song, and artists earned nothing. Spotify fixed it with a royalty rail. AI is in its Napster moment right now — and there is no Spotify." |
| 2 | 0:18–0:52 | Slide 2 (Problem). | "Every day, models get forked, fine-tuned, and merged. The New York Times is suing OpenAI. Getty is suing Stability. Stability AI went bankrupt. The EU AI Act now mandates training-data provenance. The industry has no way to track where a model came from — and no way to pay the people it was built on." |
| 3 | 0:52–1:22 | Slide 3 (Solution — the cascade). | "Mekar is that royalty rail. Every model registers as an INFT with a provable lineage. Every time it's used, the fee cascades on-chain to its ancestors — the owner, the parents it was forked from, all the way to the training-data contributors. Automatic, atomic, no middleman taking a cut." |
| 4 | 1:22–1:48 | Slide 6 (Why 0G). | "We built this on 0G because Mekar needs every part of it — 0G Chain to settle the cascade atomically, 0G Storage to anchor model weights and provenance, and 0G Compute for sealed, attested inference." |
| 5 | 1:48–2:14 | Slide 9 (Numbers) — or cut to the live explorer / chainscan. | "And this is live — not a prototype. Seven contracts on 0G mainnet. Fifty-nine tests passing. Real model data anchored on 0G Storage, verified end to end. Thirteen royalty settlements already flowing on-chain, with wei-perfect math." |
| 6 | 2:14–2:38 | Slide 10 (closing). | "The agentic economy is coming — millions of AI agents built on each other. Without a royalty rail, open AI starves. Mekar makes every inference pay it forward. Every AI has a lineage. Every inference pays its ancestors." |

VO is ~280 words — about 2:20–2:35 at a calm pace.

## Notes

- The pitch video is **optional bonus material** — the demo video is the
  mandatory one. Record the demo video first.
- Keep numbers honest and current: 7 contracts, 59 tests, 13 settlements.
  These are verifiable on `chainscan.0g.ai` — do not inflate them.
- The Napster→Spotify framing is the strongest hook; lead with it.
